Quick-start priorities

  • Keep food, water and stamina healthy before taking risks.
  • Use scent and the compass to navigate instead of sprinting blindly.
  • Learn one safe route at a time and use the live Map page to build map knowledge.
01

First 10 minutes

Get oriented before travelling far. Check your character/status screen, identify water, scent for nearby resources, and avoid burning all your stamina just because the coast looks clear.

  • Food + water + stamina are your basic survival buffer.
  • Move cautiously near water and open ground; both attract traffic.
  • If you are young, survival and diet progress matter more than winning fights.
  • Use the Map tab to learn the name of the area you are actually standing in.
02

Controls & navigation

Common default Evrima controls. Keybinds can change or be rebound, so the in-game controls menu remains authoritative.

  • Q hold: scent · Q tap: compass.
  • H: lie down/rest · hold H for the safe-log sleep flow.
  • Tab: character/status information.
  • E: interact/use · G: grab/carry or take chunks where supported.
  • 1–4: broadcast/friendly/threat/help calls on common defaults.
03

Diet & nutrients

Eating the right foods does more than fill your stomach. Nutrients influence growth and useful survival bonuses, so a balanced diet is usually worth the extra travel.

  • Watch the α / β / γ nutrient slots rather than eating only whatever is closest.
  • Better nutrient coverage improves growth and other bonuses.
  • Herbivore food availability can depend heavily on zones.
  • Do not arrive at a fight starving, dehydrated or exhausted.
04

Migration, patrol & sanctuary

These systems are navigation and resource cues, not guaranteed safety. The map overlays on this site show reference locations; live zone activation is not currently supplied by Primeval Refuge RCON.

  • Migration: especially important for herbivore diet opportunities.
  • Patrol: smaller activity/resource areas; carnivores can use them as hunting leads.
  • Sanctuary: intended to help young dinosaurs; scent for the tree-style sanctuary cue.
  • Other players know these systems too, so a useful zone can also become dangerous.
05

Growing safely

Growth is a survival objective, not an invitation to rush every encounter. Your ability to escape or absorb damage changes dramatically as you grow.

  • Juveniles should prioritise concealment, diet and safe water access.
  • Rest when it is safe; do not enter unknown terrain with an empty stamina bar.
  • As you get larger, reassess routes that were safe when you were small.
  • Consistent survival is faster than repeatedly restarting.
06

Bleeding, fractures & injuries

Do not treat every injury like ordinary HP loss. Bleeding and fractures can punish movement and turn an otherwise survivable fight into a death.

  • Running while bleeding is generally a bad idea; disengage and rest when safe.
  • Low food, water and stamina make recovery situations worse.
  • Fractures can heavily reduce mobility and need time/rest to recover.
  • Winning the fight does not matter if you bleed out afterward.
07

Combat survival

Most deaths start before the first hit: bad positioning, no stamina, no escape route, or attacking something you did not understand.

  • Know where you will retreat before committing.
  • Keep enough stamina to disengage.
  • Use terrain, water, vegetation and your species' movement strengths.
  • Do not assume a friendly call means the encounter is actually safe.
08

Mutations

Mutations let you shape a dinosaur over its lifecycle. Some options unlock through play conditions and inherited slots can come from parents.

  • Read the mutation description before locking in a choice.
  • Build around how you actually play rather than chasing a theoretical best build.
  • Later growth stages open additional lifecycle choices.
  • Mutation availability can change between game updates.
09

Nesting & raising young

Nesting is a full survival loop: court, prepare eggs, build and defend a nest, then keep hatchlings fed and alive.

  • Good diet matters because gestation and raising young consume resources.
  • Eggs and nests can attract threats.
  • Young dinosaurs inherit diet benefits through feeding.
  • Do not start a nest somewhere you cannot reliably feed, drink and defend.
10

Using the Primeval Refuge Map

The green marker is your Steam-authenticated dinosaur only. Area labels and reference-zone layers are there to teach Gateway without exposing every player on the server.

  • Names shows major Gateway area labels.
  • Migration / Patrol toggles show reference geometry.
  • Your live card reports the closest known area and reference zones containing your marker.
  • Reference overlays are not labelled LIVE unless the server can provide authoritative activation data.
Guide references checked September 2026: controls · diet · zones · sanctuary · injuries · mutations · nesting. Fan-maintained mechanics can change after game updates.
